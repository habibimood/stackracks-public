Everything a budget needs, none of the typing.

Connect your accounts once. StackRacks handles everything after that.

🏦

Every account, one place

Checking, savings, and credit cards sync automatically through Plaid. CSV import is there if a bank is being difficult.

🔎

One searchable feed

A single transaction list across all accounts. Find that $47 charge from three weeks ago in two seconds.

🏷️

Rules that learn your habits

Custom categories plus merchant rules that categorize past and future transactions. Set it once and stop thinking about it.

🔁

No double counting

Transfers and credit card payments get detected, so moving money between your own accounts never looks like spending.

🎯

Monthly budgets

Set a number per category and watch budgeted vs. actual update itself as you spend.

📊

Reports with real charts

Income, spending, net savings, top merchants, and month-over-month trends, shown as pie charts and treemaps.

📱

Web and Android

A fast React web app and a native Android app with biometric unlock.

📤

Your data, exportable

Export everything to CSV or JSON whenever you want. Yes, even back to a spreadsheet, if you really miss it.

Private by design.

It's your money. It stays yours.

Self-hosted

Runs on personal hardware and is reachable only over a private network. Not on the open internet, not someone else's cloud.

Encrypted where it counts

Passwords hashed with argon2id, bank access tokens encrypted at rest, and backups encrypted and tested.

Bank logins go through Plaid. StackRacks never sees or stores your bank credentials.

Every account, one place

Checking, savings, and credit cards sync automatically through Plaid. CSV import is there if a bank is being difficult.

🔎

One searchable feed

A single transaction list across all accounts. Find that $47 charge from three weeks ago in two seconds.

🏷️

Rules that learn your habits

Custom categories plus merchant rules that sort past and future transactions. Set it once, forget it.

🔁

No double counting

Transfers and credit card payments get detected, so moving money between your own accounts never looks like spending.

🎯

Monthly budgets

Income, spending, net savings, top merchants, and month-over-month trends, as pie charts and treemaps.

📱

Web and Android

A fast web app and a native Android app with biometric unlock.

Export everything to CSV or JSON. Yes, even back to a spreadsheet, if you really miss it.
